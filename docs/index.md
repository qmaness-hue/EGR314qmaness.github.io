## Introduction

* **This datasheet gives the design and process of creating as well as information regarding the dual-motor subsystem of team 304's EV-Scope project. It gives a more in-detail description of the motor subsystem than is in the collective team datasheet.**
### Project Summary

* The dual-motor subystem is integral to the EV-Scope, as it is the system that actually maneuvers the scope and camera in order to track and capture images of celestial objects.
* The [team report.](https://egr314-s-2026-304.github.io/) is the culmination of all three team members' works, and gives more information regarding the purpose and process for creating the EV-Scope system.
